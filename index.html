What it is

  • 300 words across 50 levels: cards, bubbles, movement breaks and a listening game.
  • Pick the child's own language, then learn any of the other six.
  • Every word is spoken first in the child's language, then in the language being learned.
  • A pet hatches from an egg after the first level and grows as the child learns; stars from levels buy toys for its room.
  • Difficulty follows the child's age: 2–4, 5–8, or 8+.
  • No ads, no subscription, no data collection. Works fully offline.
