Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Deni ("the app") is made for young children. We designed it so that it needs no personal data at all.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal information, contact details, location, photos, microphone or camera input, identifiers, or usage analytics. There is no account and no sign-in.

Data the app stores on your device

Settings (chosen languages, the child's age group, timer), progress (completed levels and words answered correctly) and the child's pet (which pet, its age, stars and room items) are saved only on the device, in the app's own storage. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app. You can clear progress in Settings at any time.

Network use

The app works fully offline. All pictures and voice recordings are included in the download. The app does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, or social network SDKs.

Purchases

Optional purchases (level packs, languages, choosing a pet, and star packs for the pet's room) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be restored through Apple in the app's Settings.

Children

The app is intended for young children, used with a grown-up. Purchases and settings sit behind a parental gate. Because no data is collected, no personal information about a child is processed, transmitted, or shared.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
