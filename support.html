How do I open the grown-up settings?

Press and hold the ⚙️ button in the corner for about one and a half seconds, then answer the simple question. A quick tap does nothing, so a child cannot get in by accident.

How do I change the languages or the age?

Settings → "Child's language", "Languages to learn", "Child's age". The age changes how many words each level has and how many pictures the finding game shows.

There is no sound

Check the volume and the side switch. The app is built to play even in silent mode. If it is still quiet, close and reopen the app.

I bought something but it is still locked

Settings → "Restore purchases", with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. The purchase is one-time and works with Family Sharing.

How does the pet work?

After the first finished level an egg hatches into a pet. It grows as your child completes new levels. Stars earned in levels buy toys and furniture for its room. A grown-up can choose a different pet or add stars behind the parental gate (the ⇄ and + buttons in the pet's room).

How do I reset progress?

Settings → Progress → "Reset progress". This clears completed levels and learned words on this device.

Does the app collect data about my child?

No. See the Privacy Policy. The app works offline and sends nothing anywhere.
